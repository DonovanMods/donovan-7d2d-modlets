# Donovan's Better Start

Adds a series of loot boxes you can choose from as your first "locate a trader" quest reward.

## Choices

1. Strength Bundle
  - T1 Baseball Bat
  - T1 Double Barrel Shotgun w/ 100 Ammo
1. Perception Bundle
  - T1 Iron Spear
  - T1 Lever-Action Rifle w/ 100 Ammo
1. Fortitude Bundle
  - T1 Iron Knuckles
  - T1 AK-47 w/ 100 Ammo
1. Agility Bundle
  - T1 Hunting Knife
  - T1 Pistol w/ 100 Ammo
1. Intellect Bundle
  - T1 Stun Baton
  - Robotic Turret w/ 100 Ammo

_All choices include a bicycle._
